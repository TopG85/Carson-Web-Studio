Service Details

Starter services

Website audit — £120–£300

A review of your current website, covering clarity, usability, accessibility, performance and conversion. You receive clear, prioritised recommendations.

Small brochure website — £295–£495

A simple website with a small number of pages and a clear, professional presentation.

Landing page — £400–£900

A focused page for a service, event, campaign or specific business objective.

Standard services

Standard custom website — £1,500–£3,000

A complete responsive website designed around your organisation, audience and goals.

Website redesign — £1,000–£3,000

A refreshed website with improved layout, navigation, structure, mobile usability and visual design.

Premium services

Larger custom website — £3,000–£5,000+

A larger project with more pages, functionality, planning and development requirements.

Strategy-led build — £3,000–£6,000+

A project including additional work on content structure, user journeys, communication goals and design direction.

Ongoing support

Monthly care plan — £40–£150 per month

Website updates, backups, security checks and small content changes after launch.

Hourly support — £50–£75 per hour

Flexible help with one-off fixes, content updates and technical tasks.

A note about pricing

These are suggested guide ranges. The final price depends on the scope of the project, the amount of content, the number of pages, required functionality and the level of support needed.
