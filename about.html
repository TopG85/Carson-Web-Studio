About

I’m Daniel, a web designer and developer and the founder of Carson Web Studio. I design and build modern, responsive websites with a focus on clarity, usability and thoughtful visual design.

My Approach

  • Clarity: Making sure visitors can easily find the information they need.
  • Visual Design: Creating layouts that are clean, balanced and engaging.
  • Usability: Building responsive websites that work well across all devices.

Mission

To help small businesses become genuinely visible and successful online, combining technical skill with creative and visual understanding — making expert-level web and SEO help accessible to people who might otherwise be priced out or overlooked by big agencies.

Core Values

  1. Empathy-led problem solving— I listen first, taking the time to properly understand a problem rather than assuming or overwhelming clients with jargon
  2. Honesty over hype — I give straight, accurate advice, and I'm upfront when something needs correcting rather than doubling down
  3. Creativity + logic combined — my photography background isn't separate from my development work; it shapes how I think about visual brands, user experience, and storytelling
  4. Growth-minded — my own career journey, from photography into software development, reflects a belief in real growth over staying comfortable
  5. Accessible expertise — I believe good technical help shouldn't be out of reach, and I care about genuinely helping people, not just billing hours

Positioning

I'm not "just another developer" or "just another photographer" — I sit at the intersection: a creative technologist who understands both how something looks and how it works. My background means I can speak to creative small businesses and photographers in a way a purely technical developer often can't — I've been in their shoes.

Unique Value Proposition

"I help creative and small businesses fix what's holding their website back — combining a developer's technical eye with a photographer's understanding of visual storytelling — so they're not just online, but actually found."

Background

Before moving into web development, I spent several years working as a natural light portrait and fashion photographer. That experience developed my eye for composition, colour and visual storytelling — skills that now influence the way I approach web design. My work and technical workflows were even featured nationally in Amateur Photographer Magazine.

I recently completed a Full Stack Software Development Diploma with Code Institute, where I developed practical skills in building responsive websites and web applications using:

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Bootstrap Python Django

Through Carson Web Studio, I enjoy helping churches, charities and community organisations create websites that clearly communicate who they are and make it easy for people to connect with them.
