Project Overview

Carb Counter is a personal-use app built to make daily insulin dosing calculations faster and more consistent. It combines a searchable food library, a meal-dose calculator, and an optional blood glucose correction calculator into a single tool, with results logged automatically so previous doses are easy to review.

  • Built-in food library with 40+ common items and their carb counts
  • Custom food entries that save and persist between sessions
  • Combined meal + correction dose calculation in one step
  • Deployed as both a native app (Expo) and a standalone web app

The Problem

  • Manually calculating carb totals and insulin doses at every meal is repetitive and time-consuming
  • Mental maths under time pressure (e.g. before eating) increases the risk of small errors
  • No easy way to track a history of doses without a paper log or spreadsheet
  • Existing apps often don't support combining a meal dose and a correction dose in one calculation

The Solution

  • A searchable, editable food library removes the need to memorise or look up carb values
  • Automatic dose calculation from a saved insulin-to-carb ratio, with rounding to 0.5 or 1.0 unit steps
  • An optional correction-dose calculator with built-in validation ranges and a high-dose warning
  • A running history log so every calculated dose is recorded automatically
  • Personal settings save automatically, so the app is ready to use immediately on return visits
